# Intent: a demo video for LinkedIn

Confirmed by the author on 2026-11-28 (interview-me). Replaces the showcase video removed in
#141, whose animated components read as a slideshow next to other demo videos made with Claude.

- **Outcome:** a polished demo video of about 35 seconds, made with Remotion from a real
  recording of the live site.
- **User:** people scrolling LinkedIn (PC flippers, recruiters, developers), many with the
  sound off.
- **Why now:** the app is finished and live; the last video didn't keep anyone's attention.
- **Content:** Try the demo, open a build, add a bundle and its parts with the price lookup, set the selling
  price and see the profit, Copy listing, then an end card with the logo and link.
- **Look:** the browser recorded at desktop size (1280 wide) in a 16:9 frame on a soft
  background, with no zoom (dropped on 2026-11-28 as too distracting); a large, clear
  cursor with a ripple on every click; a short caption for each step; the light theme, in the
  brand's warm paper and green.
- **Constraints:** the real site, not rebuilt components; recorded through "Try the demo" (no
  sign-up; log-in and the demo share a limit of 10 a minute); no sound; the Remotion code in
  its own `video/` folder, outside CI.
- **Changed on 2026-11-28:** bundles added to the content at the author's request.
- **Out of scope:** a voiceover, a phone-sized or 4:5 cut, sign-up, account pages,
  posting it.
