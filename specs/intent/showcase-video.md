# Intent: a 60-second showcase video for LinkedIn and Facebook

Confirmed by the author on 2026-11-26 (interview-me), after choosing Remotion over videowright.

- **Outcome:** a 60-second portrait MP4 (4:5, 1080×1350) of PC Flipper Toolkit, made with
  Remotion by animating the app's real UI (its phone layout), not a screen recording:
  - a hook (~5s);
  - one flip from start to finish on the demo's sold build (~40s): parts and a bundle going in
    with the checklist filling up, price lookup from EasyPC and PC Express, the selling price
    and the profit adding up to +₱4,200, copy the listing, the builds list with the month's
    totals;
  - "built by me" with the stack (~10s);
  - an end card (~5s): "Try the demo, no sign-up needed", the live link and the author's name.
- **User:** PC flippers and recruiters scrolling LinkedIn and Facebook.
- **Why now:** the app is finished and live; the author wants to show it where people already
  are.
- **Success:** a video the author would post as it is. It reads with the sound off (large
  captions) and holds each beat long enough to talk over.
- **Constraint:**
  - The video is silent: the author records the voice-over and adds any music.
  - The spec carries a draft narration script with timings, so the pacing and the voice line up.
  - Remotion lives in its own `video/` folder and never touches the app's build or deploy.
    The author's choice of Remotion is the OK for the new dependency.
- **Out of scope:** voice-over, music and sound effects; landscape or square cuts; account
  pages, Google sign-in and emails; uploading or posting it.
